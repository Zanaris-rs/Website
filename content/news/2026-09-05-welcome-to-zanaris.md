Zanaris is an <b class="green">invite-only</b> rehost of
[Lost City](https://github.com/LostCityRS). We are not its authors. We run
their work with our own fork at
[github.com/Zanaris-rs](https://github.com/Zanaris-rs). Nothing here is for
sale. There are no memberships, and there never will be.

**Got an invite? Don't sit on it.** Each link lets exactly one person in, and
it stops working after fourteen days. Open it, pick your name, and a minute
later you can be standing in Lumbridge. The game runs in your browser, so
there is nothing to download and nothing to pay.

**Get in early.** Every account gets a citizen number in the order it arrived,
and it stays on your hiscores page for good. The low numbers only go once.

**No invite yet?** Ask a friend who plays. There is no waiting list and no
sign-up form: every player here was let in by somebody who vouched for them,
which keeps the world full of people who came to play.

See you in Lumbridge.
